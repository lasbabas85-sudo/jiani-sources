## ⚠️ Critical Caveat: Post-Exercise Stretching as Recovery

**Static stretching performed at the end of training sessions is NOT an established recovery tool.**

### Evidence Summary

| Finding | Source | N | Year |
|---------|--------|---|------|
| No difference vs passive rest (force, ROM, DOMS at 24-72h) | Afonso et al. meta-analysis | 11 RCTs, 229 participants | 2021 |
| Post-exercise stretching does NOT reduce DOMS | Delphi Consensus | 20 international experts | 2025 |
| Vascular/autonomic benefits only (FMD%, HRV) | Frenda et al. RCT | 27 healthy males | 2026 |

### Practical Rules

✅ **DO:**
- Use stretching to develop flexibility as a standalone objective (hamstring, hip mobility)
- Apply post-exercise stretching IF targeting specific vascular/autonomic adaptations (Frenda et al. 42690430)
- Rely on sleep, training load management (ACWR), and active recovery for true recovery

❌ **DON'T:**
- Present static stretching as a recovery strategy for force development or DOMS reduction
- Perform prolonged static stretching (>60s/muscle) immediately before explosive/power activities
- Expect acute performance enhancement from stretching before competition

## 🎯 Key Takeaways by Topic

### Range of Motion Development
- **Best evidence:** Systematic reviews (27458757, 42539555) show consistent ROM gains with static stretching
- **Duration matters:** See 42539555 for evidence-based guidance on stretching duration
- **Long-term:** Chronic stretching reliably improves ROM (Delphi consensus, 40513717)

### Acute Performance Effects
- **Pre-exercise concern:** Static stretching >60s immediately before power activities reduces force/speed (22316148, 21373870)
- **Warm-up context:** See 21373870 for distinction between stretching in isolation vs as part of full warm-up
- **Sport-specific:** 42694020 shows PNF-CR + NMES can improve flexibility without impairing adolescent jump performance

### Recovery & Autonomic Markers
- **Limited evidence:** Only 42690430 shows post-exercise stretching benefit (vascular/HRV only)
- **Broader recovery:** For force recovery and DOMS, sleep and load management > stretching
- **Practical priority:** Focus recovery efforts on sleep (consolidation), not stretching techniques

### Mechanism & Physiology
- **Neurological responses:** 42133057 (mechanisms across populations)
- **Stiffness regulation:** 42627476 (ROM vs joint stiffness distinction)
- **Fascia/myofascial:** 37949575 (myofascial release as complementary modality)
